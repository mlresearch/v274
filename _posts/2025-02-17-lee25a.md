abstract: 'This paper introduces ARCLE, an environment designed to facilitate reinforcement
  learning research on the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus (ARC). Addressing this
  inductive reasoning benchmark with reinforcement learning presents these challenges:
  a vast action space, a hard-to-reach goal, and a variety of tasks. We demonstrate
  that an agent with proximal policy optimization can learn individual tasks through
  ARCLE. The adoption of non-factorial policies and auxiliary losses led to performance
  enhancements, effectively mitigating issues associated with action spaces and goal
  attainment. Based on these insights, we propose several research directions and
  motivations for using ARCLE, including MAML, GFlowNets, and World Models.'
